# Market symbols

Official TradingView symbol artwork downloaded on 14 October 2026 from
`https://s3-symbol-logo.tradingview.com/`.

- `apple.svg`, `microsoft.svg`, `nvidia.svg`, `tesla.svg`: same source filenames.
- `us.svg`: `country/US.svg`
- `eu.svg`: `country/EU.svg`
- `gb.svg`: `country/GB.svg`

These identify stocks and currency pairs in the decorative hero semicircle.
They do not represent a TradingView endorsement or live price feed.
